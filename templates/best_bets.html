📒 The Rule & How It Tested

“Tested” is every runner that fit the rule from February to November 2026, paid at Betfair prices after 8% commission. The rule was picked after seeing the results, so that flatters it a little. “Live” counts only picks shown on this page before the jump, from 15 Nov 2026, as win bets — that is the real test. The favourite comes from live Ladbrokes odds, so a pick can come and go as the market moves. Ratings are the analyzer, PFAI, the time rating and the class rating.
